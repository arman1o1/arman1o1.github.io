World Models & Video DiTs: 3D Causal VAEs, Spatiotemporal RoPE & Action-Conditioned Rollouts

November 26, 2026 • ☕ 12 min read

When I tested Gemini Omni in Making a Still Bird Fly and derived continuous-time transport in Flow Matching & Diffusion Transformers (DiT), one engineering question loomed over everything: how do you scale a 2D image DiT to coherent 3D video – and then turn a passive video generator into an interactive, action-conditioned World Model? Three architectural pillars make it work: a 3D Causal VAE, Factorized 3D RoPE, and Diffusion Forcing (CogVideoX, Yang et al., 2024, arXiv:2408.06072).

1. The 3D Causal VAE & Spatiotemporal Compression

A 5-second 720p video at 24 FPS has $120 \times 720 \times 1280 \times 3 \approx 332\text{ million}$ pixel values. A 3D Causal VAE compresses this by $4\times$ temporally and $8\times 8$ spatially (a $256\times$ reduction) into a latent tube $\mathbf{z} \in \mathbb{R}^{C \times (1 + (T-1)/4) \times (H/8) \times (W/8)}$. Crucially, frame 0 is compressed independently as a standalone image while every subsequent temporal convolution uses causal (left-only) padding:

Causal 3D Latent Compression & Per-Frame Diffusion Forcing Velocity Loss
$$\mathbf{z}_t = \mathcal{E}_{\text{causal}}(\mathbf{x}_{0:t}), \qquad \mathbf{z}_t^{(\tau_t)} = (1 - \tau_t)\mathbf{z}_t + \tau_t \boldsymbol{\epsilon}_t, \quad \tau_t \stackrel{\text{i.i.d.}}{\sim} \mathrm{LogitNormal}(0, 1)$$ $$\mathcal{L}_{\text{DF}} = \mathbb{E}\!\left[\sum_{t=1}^{L} \left\| v_\theta\!\left(\mathbf{z}_{1:L}^{(\boldsymbol{\tau})},\, \boldsymbol{\tau},\, \mathbf{a}_{1:L}\right)_t - (\boldsymbol{\epsilon}_t - \mathbf{z}_t) \right\|_2^2\right]$$

Notice two superpowers here: (1) Because frame 0 is encoded causally without future lookahead, the exact same VAE and DiT seamlessly train on mixed batches of still images ($T=1$) and videos ($T=49$). (2) Giving every frame $t$ its own independent noise level $\tau_t \in [0, 1]$ (Diffusion Forcing) lets the model keep clean past history frames at $\tau_{\lt t} \approx 0$ while denoising future frames $\tau_t \to 0$ conditioned on user actions $\mathbf{a}_t$ (keyboard/camera pose) – turning a video DiT into a real-time interactive World Model!

Replacing shared global timesteps with per-frame noise levels τ_t bridges bidirectional Video DiTs and autoregressive interactive World Models.
PyTorch Implementation: CausalConv3d & Factorized 3D RoPEPyTorch 
import torch
import torch.nn as nn
import torch.nn.functional as F

class CausalConv3d(nn.Module):
    """Left-only temporal padding so frame t never leaks information from future frames t+1."""
    def __init__(self, c_in: int, c_out: int, kt: int = 3, ks: int = 3, st: int = 1, ss: int = 1):
        super().__init__()
        self.pad_t = kt - 1
        self.pad_s = ks // 2
        self.conv = nn.Conv3d(c_in, c_out, (kt, ks, ks), stride=(st, ss, ss), padding=0)

    def forward(self, x: torch.Tensor) -> torch.Tensor:
        # x: [B, C, T, H, W] -> F.pad order is (W_l, W_r, H_l, H_r, T_l, T_r)
        x = F.pad(x, (self.pad_s, self.pad_s, self.pad_s, self.pad_s, self.pad_t, 0), mode="replicate")
        return self.conv(x)

def apply_3d_rope(q: torch.Tensor, freqs_t, freqs_h, freqs_w):
    """Factorized 3D RoPE: splits head_dim into (d_t, d_h, d_w) orthogonal rotary subspaces."""
    dt, dh, dw = freqs_t.shape[-1] * 2, freqs_h.shape[-1] * 2, freqs_w.shape[-1] * 2
    qt, qh, qw = q.split([dt, dh, dw], dim=-1)
    def rot(x, f):
        x1, x2 = x[..., 0::2], x[..., 1::2]
        c, s = f.cos(), f.sin()
        return torch.stack([x1 * c - x2 * s, x1 * s + x2 * c], dim=-1).flatten(-2)
    return torch.cat([rot(qt, freqs_t), rot(qh, freqs_h), rot(qw, freqs_w)], dim=-1)
World-Model Exposure Bias Footgun: If you condition autoregressive rollouts on strictly clean ($\tau_{\lt t} = 0$) history frames, tiny per-frame VAE reconstruction errors compound into gray mush within 30 frames. Adding a small history noise augmentation ($\tau_{\lt t} \approx 0.05$–$0.10$) during both training and rollout teaches the DiT to project slightly drifted frames back onto the natural video manifold!
