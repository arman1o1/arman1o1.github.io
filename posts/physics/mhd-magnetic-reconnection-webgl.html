2D Magnetohydrodynamics (MHD) & Magnetic Reconnection at 60 FPS

November 26, 2026 • ☕ 11 min read

If you fuse our Navier–Stokes fluid solver with Maxwell's equations, you get Magnetohydrodynamics (MHD) – the physics of electrically conducting plasma that powers solar flares, tokamak disruptions, and relativistic accretion disks. In ideal MHD ($\eta = 0$), Alfvén's theorem freezes magnetic field lines permanently into the fluid. Add even a tiny electrical resistivity $\eta \gt 0$ inside a thin current sheet, and field lines can break and reconnect – violently converting stored magnetic tension $B^2/(2\mu_0)$ into kinetic Alfvén jets.

1. Exact $\nabla\cdot\mathbf{B} = 0$ via the Magnetic Flux Function $A_z$

The biggest numerical headache in 3D MHD is preserving the solenoidal constraint $\nabla\cdot\mathbf{B} = 0$ (no magnetic monopoles). In 2D incompressible MHD, introducing the scalar out-of-plane vector potential $A_z(x,y,t)$ satisfies $\nabla\cdot\mathbf{B} = 0$ identically to machine precision:

2D Incompressible Resistive MHD & Sweet–Parker Reconnection Rate
$$\mathbf{B} = \nabla \times (A_z \hat{\mathbf{z}}) = \left(\frac{\partial A_z}{\partial y},\, -\frac{\partial A_z}{\partial x}\right), \qquad J_z = -\nabla^2 A_z$$ $$\frac{\partial A_z}{\partial t} + \mathbf{u}\cdot\nabla A_z = \eta \nabla^2 A_z, \qquad \frac{\partial \omega}{\partial t} + \mathbf{u}\cdot\nabla\omega = \mathbf{B}\cdot\nabla J_z + \nu\nabla^2\omega$$ $$\frac{v_{\text{in}}}{v_A} \sim S^{-1/2}, \qquad S = \frac{L v_A}{\eta} \;\;(\text{Lundquist number})$$

At high Lundquist numbers ($S \gt 10^4$), the elongated Sweet–Parker current sheet itself becomes tearing-unstable and shatters into a chain of self-similar magnetic islands (plasmoids), driving fast reconnection independent of $\eta$.

Why Field-Line Contours Are Free: Because $\mathbf{B}\cdot\nabla A_z = (\partial_y A_z)(\partial_x A_z) - (\partial_x A_z)(\partial_y A_z) \equiv 0$, every level set $A_z(x,y) = \text{const}$ is an exact magnetic field line! Plotting isolines of $A_z$ over the current density heatmap $J_z = -\nabla^2 A_z$ immediately exposes every X-point (reconnection site) and O-point (magnetic island).
