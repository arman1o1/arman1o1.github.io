In The 2D XY Model & BKT Vortex Unbinding, equilibrium spins aligned smoothly everywhere except at point-like topological vortices. Replace thermal noise with non-local phase-lagged coupling among identical oscillators (Kuramoto & Battogtokh, 2002), and symmetry shatters spontaneously into a Chimera State: an impossible-looking hybrid where perfectly phase-locked spiral waves surround a macroscopic, boiling core of completely desynchronized oscillators – even though every single oscillator obeys the exact same equation!

1. The 2D Non-Local Kuramoto–Sakaguchi Equation

Each pixel $\mathbf{r}$ carries a phase $\theta(\mathbf{r}, t) \in [0, 2\pi)$ coupled to all neighbors within a disk of radius $R$ with phase frustration parameter $\alpha \in [0, \pi/2)$:

Non-Local Kuramoto–Sakaguchi Phase Evolution & Local Order Parameter
$$\frac{\partial \theta(\mathbf{r}, t)}{\partial t} = \omega + \frac{K}{|B_R|} \iint_{|\mathbf{r}' - \mathbf{r}| \le R} \sin\!\big(\theta(\mathbf{r}', t) - \theta(\mathbf{r}, t) - \alpha\big)\, d^2\mathbf{r}'$$ $$Z(\mathbf{r}, t) = R_{\text{loc}}(\mathbf{r}, t)\, e^{i\Phi(\mathbf{r}, t)} = \frac{1}{|B_R|} \iint_{|\mathbf{r}' - \mathbf{r}| \le R} e^{i\theta(\mathbf{r}', t)}\, d^2\mathbf{r}'$$

When $\alpha = 0$, the system minimizes the XY energy and locks into global synchronization ($R_{\text{loc}} \to 1$). As $\alpha \to \pi/2 \approx 1.57$, the coupling becomes nearly conservative/repulsive near spiral cores: oscillators inside the core experience a vanished mean field $R_{\text{loc}} \approx 0$ and drift chaotically while oscillators outside the core remain phase-locked in rotating spiral arms.
